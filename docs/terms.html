Using Spendbrake

Spendbrake helps you plan purchases. Category suggestions, “do you need it?” prompts and price information are for guidance only. They aren't financial advice, and the decisions about what to buy are yours.

Price information

Prices found by Spendbrake Pro come from public web search results. They can be out of date, incomplete or wrong. Always confirm the price, product and delivery costs with the retailer before you buy.

Spendbrake Pro subscription

  • Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, billed through your App Store or Google Play account.
  • It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel anytime in your store account settings.
  • Pro includes a monthly allowance of price checks, shown in the app. Unused checks don't carry over.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

On iOS, Apple's standard End User License Agreement also applies.
