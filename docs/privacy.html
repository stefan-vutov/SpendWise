Privacy policy

SpendWise · Last updated 26 November 2026

SpendWise is a purchase planner. We built it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens to your information in the free version and in the optional SpendWise Pro subscription.

Free version

  • Your list stays on your phone. Item names, prices, notes, categories and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive them.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Reminders are created on your phone. We don't use push notification servers.
  • “Search stores for this” opens a web search in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies.

SpendWise Pro (optional subscription)

If you subscribe, these features need to send some information to our server:

  • Price checks and AI suggestions: the names, notes and target prices of the items being checked, plus the country and currency you chose, are sent to our server. Our server uses Anthropic's AI service, which searches the web for current prices. We don't send your name, email or contacts.
  • Subscription: payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. It links your purchase to a random, anonymous app user ID.
  • Usage limits: our server stores how many price checks that anonymous ID has used this month, so we can apply the monthly allowance.
  • Price results may be kept for up to 24 hours, without anything that identifies you, so repeated checks of the same product are faster.

We use this information only to provide the features. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Service providers

  • Anthropic: AI processing for Pro features (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat: subscription status (privacy policy)
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and payments

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. To delete Pro usage records linked to your anonymous ID, email us and we'll remove them.

Children

SpendWise isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
